# ADR Severity & Case Classification

## Objective

To classify Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) based on severity, seriousness, and expectedness according to standard pharmacovigilance guidelines.

---

# Case 1

## Patient Details

- Age: 45 Years
- Gender: Female

## Suspected Drug

Amoxicillin 500 mg

## Adverse Drug Reaction

- Skin rash
- Itching
- Red patches

### Severity Assessment

**Moderate**

Reason:
The patient required discontinuation of the suspected drug and treatment with an antihistamine.

### Seriousness Classification

**Non-Serious**

Reason:
- No hospitalization
- No permanent disability
- No life-threatening condition
- Patient recovered completely

### Expectedness

**Expected**

Reason:
Skin rash is a well-known adverse reaction of Amoxicillin.

# Conclusion

Severity assessment determines the intensity of the adverse reaction, while seriousness determines its clinical outcome. Proper classification helps healthcare professionals prioritize patient safety and improve pharmacovigilance reporting.
